About

OpenVibe is a network of small, complete products — streams, chat, games, hosting, a wiki — that sign you in once and know about each other. Each one is built in the open by OpenVibers.

Under them sit the parts the sites share: one identity and wallet, an event bus, a source registry, a media service and a small set of contracts that keep every interface honest.
