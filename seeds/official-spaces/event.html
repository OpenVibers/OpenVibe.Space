Once a month, whoever builds on OpenVibe gets together in one room: what shipped, what broke, what's next. Free, online, no recording.

Who's coming

    Questions

    Where does it happen?

    In the OpenVibe Chat server, in #community-night. The room opens 15 minutes before.

    Do I have to be a developer?

    No. If you use OpenVibe and have an opinion about it, you belong in the room.

    Is it recorded?

    No. Notes and decisions are written up afterwards on the blog.
